# Vessel Archive × Pick-n-Pull

A six-section pitch positioning Vessel as an outsourced alternative to the social media hire, with content production, channel management, targeted website fixes and Meta ads.

Proposed 90-day Sacramento pilot: two yards, two shoot days and 12 original videos per month. Media spend and creator fees are separate. Final channel coverage and response hours are agreed at kickoff. No pricing or performance guarantees.

Live: https://antoniemykallewis-source.github.io/pick-n-pull-pitch/

The portable index.html embeds its styles, scripts, fonts and imagery. No build step is required for hosting. Navigate by scrolling, chapter selector or arrow keys. The audit pairs six observed problems with practical fixes in two keyboard-accessible tabs. Three sample video concepts include clickable scripts. The close connects the fixes, content and ads to a month-by-month plan.

The audit covers public customer journeys and visible profile information as of October 2026. It does not claim authenticated analytics, completed vehicle quotes, verified current follower totals or a measured performance score. Website implementation is coordinated with Pick-n-Pull's web team. Research references are kept out of the client-facing deck.

Vessel and Pick-n-Pull assets remain with their respective owners. This is an independent proposal, not an existing client engagement.
